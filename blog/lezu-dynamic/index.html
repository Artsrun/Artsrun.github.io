Language as a dynamic system

12 Sep 2026 · lezu-dynamic v1.2

language = existence + change + time + social repetition

A living language is not a warehouse of words. It is a roof under which four loads keep moving: what exists, what is happening, how it changes in time, and who repeats it until the form holds.

Stability is not the absence of motion. Stability is a temporary organization of change. When the floor cracks, the table is not guilty. The arrangement broke.

Two rooms sit under the same roof. The philosophical room names existence and action. The grammatical room names suffixes and verb classes. A corridor joins them: a grammatical rule is not a philosophical model. Same house. Two laws.

Usage does not wait for the rule. The rule is born from use, and only after enough social repetition does a form start looking like it was always there.

The diagnostic path is a reading tool, not a historical law. See the sketch and the Fool’s Journey for the same sentence told in faces.
