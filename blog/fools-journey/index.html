foul / fool · one letter of rot inside the walker

Fourteen faces. Four loops: open, turn, fall, return. Each beat names an existence and a change. The path keys are diagnostic — they tag a reading of the walk, they do not legislate history.

Beat 02 invents a plan that is only brilliant inside the face. Beat 13 reuses that face with the sparkle deleted. Beat 04 walks forward as if motion were proof. Beat 14 walks the same walk. Relapse is visible only if you keep an archive.

The table flips at beat 09 and stands again at beat 12. Form is restored. Habit is not. That is what “temporary organization of change” looks like when it wears arms and eyes.

The cycle is fourteen acts. The lexicon behind it is ten verbs. Extra joy-mouths are handwriting.
